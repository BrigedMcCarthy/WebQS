# QS-247 Stirling Converter Fission Reactor - Operations Guide

**Quantum Science-Certified*** Simple Guide

This is your comprehensive guide on how to efficiently and safely operate the QS-247 Stirling Converter Fission Reactor. This document includes specifications, operational procedures, potential abnormalities, and emergency protocols.

## Location

The QS-247 Stirling Converter Fission Reactor, otherwise also known as **Project Helios**, is located in the **Foxtrot-9 Area**, which is located directly in front of Station 012 02, right above the Ventilation Annex.

**Quick Access:** Enter the Maintenance Elevator and go to Floor 3. You will be right next to the entrance to the Foxtrot-9 Area.

## Features

### System Components

#### Thermal Loop Pumps, Inlets and Outlets
Exactly the same thing as the coolant pumps for the DMR. If enabled and open, they cool the Fission Reactor. **Note:** They are locked unless the Reactor Safety System is disabled.

#### Continuous CR Transit
If Enabled, it will allow the movement of the Control Rod insertion to not be stopped after 1 second and be constant.

#### Control Rod Speed
- **1x setting:** Makes the Control Rods move at 1x speed
- **2x setting:** Moves them at 2x speed

#### Reactor Safety System
⚠️ **DO NOT DISABLE IT.** This is a QAC+ rank locked switch. If disabled, it will prevent the Reactor from activating Auto-SCRAM if the Pressure or Temperature is too high, making it easy to accidentally blow it up.

#### Rod Controls
Gives you the option to move individual Control Rods if you want to be extra precise.

#### UP/DOWN Lever
Controls the movement of the Control Rods:
- **UP position:** Raises the Control Rods, increases pressure, Neutron Flux and temperature
- **DOWN position:** Lowers the CRs and progressively lowers pressure and temperature

#### Reactor Overview
Displays the temperature and pressure of the different sectors of the Reactor as well as the currently selected Control Rods.

#### Converters
They generate electricity. They also reduce the Pressure by around 600-800 PSI when connected to the Grid.

#### Relief Valves
If switched on, it will lower the pressure of the selected sector of the Reactor.

#### Synchroscope
Indicates whether the electricity generated by the Fission Reactor is synchronized with the Grid:
- **Hovering over Zero:** Electricity is at 60 Hz and able to be synchronized with the Grid
- **Lagging or Leading:** Fission Reactor will not be able to be synchronized with the Grid

## Mechanics

### Temperature
- The higher it is, the better (to a degree)
- Can also increase the Pressure if it's high
- **Critical:** If it goes above around 850°C, the Thermal Power alarm will activate
- Auto-SCRAM will trigger shortly after

### Pressure
- **The most fragile and most important mechanic** of the Reactor
- Safe operating range: Below 3500 PSI
- **Warning:** Above 3700 PSI triggers Auto-SCRAM
- **Danger:** Above 4000 PSI = **Reactor explosion**

### Neutron Flux
- Very important mechanic that if not kept under supervision will cause chaos
- Always at 0% baseline
- **Increases** every time you raise the Control Rods
- **Returns to zero** as soon as you stop raising the CRs
- **Main danger:** Increases Pressure rapidly (can jump from 1% to 12% before you notice)

## Recommended Normal Operations

### Cold Start Procedure

1. Ensure that the **Reactor Safety System switch is ON**
2. Ensure that the **Thermal Loop is ON** and its **Inlets and Outlets are also set to ON**
3. Ensure that the **Control Rod Speed is set to 1x**
4. Set the **Continuous CR Transit switch to ON**
5. Set **all Converter Pistons to ON**
6. Switch **all Relief Valves to OFF**
7. Select **Global Rod Selection** next to the CR UP/DOWN lever
8. Move the **UP/DOWN lever to the UP position**
9. Closely monitor the **Neutron Flux** on the System Overview screen
10. Move the **UP/DOWN lever to the Neutral position** as soon as Neutron Flux reaches 8%
11. Once Neutron Flux drops down to around 0%, move the **UP/DOWN lever to the UP position**
12. Repeat steps 8-11 until:
    - Control Rod Insertion reaches 35%
    - Average Pressure is around or above 3000 PSI
    - Temperature is around 600-700°C and rising

**Decision Point:**

- **If:** Pressure reached around or over 3000 PSI and Temperature crossed over 500°C
  - → Skip to Step 14

- **If:** Control Rod Insertion approaches 35% and/or the Temperature is staying below 400-500°C and the Pressure does not reach 2800 PSI
  - → Do NOT go below 30% CR Insertion
  - → Wait a moment for the Reactor to heat up

13. When the Average Temperature reaches 800°C, set the CR Insertion to around 35%
    - Remember to monitor the Pressure while doing this step
14. Set the **Continuous CR Transit to OFF position**

### Auxiliary Grid Connection Procedure

**READ FIRST:** The Fission Reactor can be connected **ONLY** to the **AUXILIARY Grid**. If the currently used Grid is Primary or External, this Procedure is to be ignored.

**Decision Point:**

- **If:** The Reactor is currently abiding by the MtRO Procedure
  - → Lower the CR Insertion to 35%
  - → Wait for the Total Power Output to stabilize at 40k kW

- **If:** Fission Reactor is already stabilized at around 40k kW
  - → Skip to Step 2

**Connection Steps:**

1. When the Power Output of every Converter reaches 10000 kW (or 40k kW for the Total Power Output), set the CR Insertion to 35%, or whichever value is deemed stable enough to keep the Power Output stable and consistent.
2. Carefully line up the Synchroscope needle with ZERO value by Opening and Closing the Relief Valves to balance the Power Output of the Converters.
3. When the Synchroscope needle gently hovers above ZERO value with "Same Speed" lamp illuminating, press the **Synchronize with Grid button**.

### Maintaining the Reactor Operations (MtRO) Procedure

1. **Monitor the Average Pressure**
   - Keep it above 3000 PSI at all times
   - Exception: If the Fission Reactor is connected to the Grid, the Pressure will be considerably lower (by approx. 600-800 PSI) even when generating Maximum Total Power
   - If it rises above 3500 PSI, set the appropriate Relief Valves to ON position as long as necessary

2. **Monitor the Temperature**
   - Keep it around or above 750°C at all times
   - If it falls below 750°C, temporarily raise the CR Insertion up to 30% to heat up the Reactor
   - Carefully monitor the Pressure while doing this step, and use Relief Valves if necessary
   - If it rises above 875°C, lower the Control Rods if deemed necessary
   - Alternatively: If that does not lower the Temperature enough, set the Relief Valves to ON position

3. **Keep the Control Rods at 35% Insertion** with a MoE** of ±5%
   - Adjust the Control Rods Insertion within that margin whenever deemed necessary by other factors (Pressure, Temperature)
   - Do NOT lower the CR Insertion below 30% to avoid volatile conditions and accidental Auto-SCRAM triggering

4. **Ensure proper power generation**
   - Reactor should generate around 40-50k kW of power
   - If it does not, ensure all Converter Pistons are connected and Temperature is around 800°C

5. **Repeat steps 1-4** for the duration of Reactor's Operation

### Shutdown Procedures

#### Automatic Shutdown
1. Press the **Reactor SCRAM button**
2. After Temperature and Pressure is reduced to approx. 75°C and 1060 PSI respectively, press the **SCRAM Reset button**

#### Manual Shutdown
1. Set the **Continuous CR Switch to ON**
2. Set the **Control Rod Speed to 2x**
3. Set **all Relief Valves to ON**
4. Move the **UP/DOWN Lever to DOWN position**
5. **Disconnect** the Fission Reactor from the Grid once Power Output of Converters goes below 1125 kW
6. Leave the controls like that until the Pressure reaches approx. 1060 PSI and the Temperature cools down to 75°C

## Abnormal Operations

### High Neutron Flux (>8%)

1. Immediately set the **UP/DOWN Lever to the Neutral Position**
2. If reacted to after the Average Pressure went over 3200 PSI
   - → Expect Auto SCRAM

### High System Pressure

1. Set the **appropriate Relief Valves to ON**
2. Set the **Relief Valves to OFF** once the Average Pressure (or the Pressure of a specific Sector) is back to the recommended amounts listed in MtRO Procedure
3. Ensure that the **UP/DOWN Lever is set to the Neutral position**

### High Thermal Power

1. Lower the Control Rods with the UP/DOWN Lever if deemed necessary
2. Ensure that the **Thermal Loop** along with **all of its Inlets and Outlets are all Enabled and Open**
3. Temporarily Open the Relief Valves if deemed necessary

### Temperature Imbalance

**READ FIRST:** This Procedure can be applied **ONLY** when the Reactor Safety System is **DISABLED** AND if there is a Supervisor present to use the Coolant systems. If it is **ENABLED**, this Procedure is to be ignored.

**NOTE:** Temperature Imbalance Procedure applies **ONLY** when the difference between two most contrasting sectors of the Fission Reactor is at ±300°C or more. Differences below that threshold are natural and nominal.

**Decision Point:**

- **If:** The hotter part of the Fission Reactor is close to or above 800°C
  - → Proceed to the Shutdown Procedures
  - → Restart the Fission Reactor using the Cold Start Procedure

- **If:** The hotter part of the Fission Reactor is lower than 700°C
  - → Close the Coolant Outlet of the colder part
  - → Ensure that the Coolant Inlet and Outlet of the hotter part are Open
  - → Open the Coolant Outlet of the colder part once it has the same Temperature as the hotter part

### Control Rod Fault

**Decision Point:**

- **If:** Stuck Control Rod or Rods are at or above 35% Insertion
  - → Depending on the exact insertion level of the working CRs, Lower the working Control Rods to insertion levels deemed necessary to compensate for the effects of the stuck CRs
  - → If stuck CRs do not pose any danger, continue following the MtRO Procedure
  - → If they do pose any danger, proceed to emergency shutdown

- **If:** Stuck Control Rod or Rods are below 35% Insertion
  - → Attempt to unjam the CRs by doing the Automatic Shutdown Procedure
  - → Try to manually lower the global CR Insertion level
  - → Keep attempting that until the stuck CRs get unjammed

### Reactor Safety Disabled

1. Check if there is someone on the Administration Team in the game

**Decision Point:**

- **If:** There is an Admin on an Administration Team in the game

- **If:** There are no Admins on the Administration Team in the game
  - → Check if any player on the server is a QAC member
  - → If not, perhaps it's an advanced exploiter or a QAC member that was online before they logged off

⚠️ **Critical Warning:** Pay very close attention to Pressure and Temperature when operating the Fission Reactor and do not hesitate on activating SCRAM in case something appears to go wrong.

### Overpressure Emergency

**If Pressure approaches or exceeds 3700 PSI after Auto SCRAM has been activated:**

1. **Close the Vault Door IMMEDIATELY**
2. Monitor the Pressure on the System Overview screen to ensure that it's not going to cross 4000 PSI

**Decision Point:**

- **If:** Pressure is not crossing or will not cross over 4000 PSI
  - → Wait for the Auto SCRAM to lower the Pressure to 1000 PSI
  - → Begin the Cold Start Procedure when Temperature cools down to 75°C

- **If:** Pressure crossed 4000 PSI with all annunciator lamps lighting up or is predicted to do so
  - → **Shield yourself from Explosion**
  - → **IMMEDIATELY EVAC** from the Foxtrot-9 Area in **less than 30 SECONDS** from the explosion
  - → **ELSE DEATH WILL OCCUR**

### Meltdown Emergency

**If Temperature approaches 1200°C after Auto SCRAM has been activated:**

**Decision Point:**

- **If:** Temperature is not crossing or will not cross over 1300°C
  - → Wait for the Auto SCRAM to lower the Pressure to 1060 PSI
  - → Begin the Cold Start Procedure when Temperature cools down to 75°C

- **If:** Temperature either crossed 1300°C with all annunciator lamps lighting up, an unknown noise is heard coming from the Fission Reactor itself, or one/all of them is predicted to happen
  - → **EVAC IMMEDIATELY** FROM THE FOXTROT-9 AREA
  - → **IN LESS THAN 20 SECONDS**, counted from hearing an unknown noise
  - → **OR IN 15 SECONDS** from verifying that there is glowing matter at the bottom of the reactor pool
  - → **DO NOT STAY BEHIND** due to high risk of explosion
  - → **DO NOT ENTER** the Fission Reactor itself
